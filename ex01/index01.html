Why learn programming?

Cognitive reasons

Problem-solving skills

Programming helps develop problem-solving skills and an engineering mindset.
It also aids in learning mathematics and physics.

Programming does not merely provide information; it fosters a specific way of thinking. This way of thinking is especially useful in science.

Material reasons

We shouldn't leave financial factors out of the equation. To continue learning and progressing effectively in this field, there needs to be a good financial return.

If I worked in a field other than programming, I might spend eight hours a day doing something I dislike.
I could come home exhausted and have no time left to learn.
